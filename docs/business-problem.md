
# Business Problem

SLIMTA operates across multiple e-commerce sales channels, including Amazon, Shopify, Noon, and Trendyol.

Managing inventory, orders, and operational information across these platforms creates challenges as the business grows.

Zoho Inventory serves as the central inventory management system. However, maintaining consistent stock quantities across connected marketplaces requires reliable synchronization and integration workflows.

## Key Business Challenges

### 1. Inventory Inconsistency

Products are managed centrally in Zoho Inventory but sold through multiple marketplaces.

Each marketplace maintains its own product listings and inventory information.

Without reliable synchronization, stock quantities can become inconsistent between the central inventory system and individual sales channels.

### 2. Manual Inventory Updates

Updating stock quantities separately across marketplaces is:

- Time-consuming
- Prone to human error
- Difficult to maintain consistently
- Increasingly complex as product variations and sales channels grow

Manual processes also make it harder to respond quickly to inventory changes.

### 3. Risk of Overselling

When stock updates are delayed, marketplaces may continue displaying quantities that are no longer available.

This creates risks such as:

- Overselling
- Order cancellations
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Additional operational workload
- Potential marketplace penalties

Reliable inventory synchronization is important for reducing these risks.

### 4. Fragmented Business Data

Order and inventory information is distributed across different business systems.

This includes:

- Marketplace orders
- Inventory quantities
- Sales performance
- Product-level activity
- Marketplace-specific operational information

Reviewing this information separately makes it difficult to obtain a consolidated view of business performance.

### 5. Limited Operational Visibility

Business operators need a centralized way to monitor:

- Inventory availability and low-stock conditions
- Orders across sales channels
- Revenue and marketplace performance
- Integration status
- Synchronization activity
- System errors and operational alerts

Without a consolidated dashboard, identifying problems and monitoring business activity requires checking multiple systems.

## Project Objective

The objective was to develop an AWS-based inventory automation and operational monitoring platform that works alongside Zoho Inventory.

The platform was designed to:

- Maintain Zoho Inventory as the primary inventory authority.
- Synchronize operational inventory data into AWS.
- Support automated marketplace inventory and order workflows.
- Consolidate order and inventory information in a management dashboard.
- Provide business intelligence and marketplace performance visibility.
- Improve troubleshooting through centralized logging and operational alerts.
- Reduce dependence on repetitive manual processes.

## Expected Business Value

The project aims to improve inventory consistency, reduce manual operational work, provide clearer visibility into marketplace activity, and support more reliable day-to-day e-commerce operations.

The platform does not replace Zoho Inventory. Instead, it extends the existing business system through AWS-based automation, integrations, and monitoring.
